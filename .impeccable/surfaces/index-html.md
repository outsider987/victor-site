# Surface brief: portfolio landing (index.html)

Scope: the single scroll-story landing page. Visitor mode: **Experience** (portfolio), with Persuade duties: role, proof and contact must be legible in seconds.

Audience and job: hiring managers and business clients, weighted equally. They skim, decide whether Victor can build what they need, then take the résumé, email or a live link.

Proof and content: seven user-selected works (CypherLab, Mediconcen, 3C換金所, 天鳳宮, Sandstorm God, BetCorgi, Ironvale) shown through real screens; skills; the AI collaboration method; experience; contact. English primary, 中文 toggle.

Constraints: all content readable without WebGL; reduced motion honoured; confidential detail withheld per PRODUCT.md; GitHub Pages under /victor-site/.

Memorable moment: Victor's clay figure walks the road, a station builds itself frame by frame, and the camera pushes into the work until it fills the screen.

User steer (2026-09-27): when the figure arrives at a station, the work and its explanation must be shown large.

Unresolved: none blocking. Personal domain undecided (stays on GitHub Pages).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a stop-motion clay set. Victor's clay self walks one tabletop road; each work is a station that builds itself as he arrives. Refuses the dark-space, particle, glow-text three.js portfolio and the card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Fingerprinted plasticine under warm key light: teal night backdrop, tomato, butter, moss, plum clay; cream paper tags pinned with clay pins carry every word; clay beads on a string are the navigation. Characters and props move on twos; camera and type stay smooth.

STORY: Visitors meet Victor, learn in one line what he builds, then walk with him through seven works, the workbench of skills, the AI crew that helps him ship, and the road so far, ending on "that's a wrap" with contact and résumés.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed clay set at dusk. Clay Victor stands under a lamp right of centre at figure scale. Left: pinned paper tag with "Victor Chang", the role line beneath it, a one-line promise, primary "See the works", secondary "Get in touch", résumé link. Top: slim nav with EN/中文. Bottom: bead string of stations.

Signature interaction: Arrive and build. Scroll walks him on twos to the next station; its props pop in with squash; the camera pushes in until the work's clay-framed screen fills about 60% of the viewport while a large paper tag unfolds with the story. Scrolling back unbuilds in reverse.

FORM: Impeccable's pick (grounded #1, the character journey), re-materialised as claymation at the user's steer after a style round; seed 1c7cacc2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
